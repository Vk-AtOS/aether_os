Privacy Policy

VKatOS  ·  Last updated: July 2026  ·  Effective: July 1, 2026

Short version: We collect only what we need to operate the platform. We do not sell your data. We do not use your data for advertising. You can request deletion at any time by emailing velan@vkatos.com.

1. Who we are

VKatOS is an AI-native developer infrastructure company incorporated in India. Our registered office is in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India. We operate the AetherOS and DCSP platforms accessible at vkatos.com and its subdomains.

This Privacy Policy explains how VKatOS collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal data when you use our services. It applies to all visitors, registered users, and customers of VKatOS products.

2. Data we collect

We collect only the data necessary to provide and improve our services:

  • Account data: your name, email address, and (optionally) company name when you register for an account.
  • Usage analytics: page views, feature interactions, session duration, and error logs — used to understand how people use the platform and where we should improve.
  • Pipeline inputs: application specifications and configuration you submit to AetherOS or DCSP to generate software. These are used solely to execute your build and are not shared with third parties.
  • Billing data: payment method details processed by our payment provider (Stripe). VKatOS never stores full card numbers on our servers.
  • Technical logs: IP addresses, browser type, and timestamps — retained for up to 90 days for security and reliability purposes.

We do not collect sensitive personal data (health information, government IDs, biometrics) and we do not knowingly collect data from individuals under 18 years of age.

3. How we use your data

Your data is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Provisioning and operating your account and subscriptions.
  • Executing AI pipeline runs (AetherOS) and CI repair jobs (DCSP) that you initiate.
  • Processing payments and issuing invoices.
  • Sending transactional emails — account confirmation, password resets, build notifications. We do not send unsolicited marketing email without your explicit opt-in.
  • Analysing aggregate usage patterns to improve platform performance and reliability.
  • Detecting and preventing abuse, fraud, or security incidents.

We do not use your data to train AI models, do not build advertising profiles, and do not engage in automated decision-making that produces legal or significant effects on you without human review.

4. Data sharing and third parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party. Data is shared only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: infrastructure partners (cloud hosting, database) who process data on our behalf under strict data processing agreements.
  • Payment processors: Stripe processes payment information under their own privacy policy and PCI-DSS compliance programme.
  • Legal obligation: if we are required by law, court order, or governmental authority to disclose data, we will do so — and where legally permitted, we will notify you first.

All third-party processors we use are contractually bound to use your data only as instructed by VKatOS and to maintain appropriate security standards.

5. Data retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. Specifically:

  • Account data is retained until you request deletion or for up to 2 years after account inactivity.
  • Pipeline artifacts and generated code are retained for 90 days after a run completes, after which they are automatically purged.
  • Billing records are retained for 7 years to comply with Indian accounting and tax law.
  • Technical logs are retained for 90 days and then deleted.

6. Data security

We implement industry-standard security controls to protect your personal data:

  • All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher.
  • Data at rest is encrypted using AES-256 encryption.
  • Access to customer data is restricted to authorised VKatOS personnel on a need-to-know basis.
  • We conduct regular security reviews and penetration tests.
  • We maintain an incident response plan; in the event of a data breach affecting your personal data, we will notify you within 72 hours where required by applicable law.

7. Your rights

As a data subject, you have the following rights over your personal data:

  • Access: request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: request that inaccurate or incomplete data be corrected.
  • Deletion: request that your personal data be deleted ("right to be forgotten"), subject to legal retention obligations.
  • Portability: request your data in a machine-readable format for transfer to another service.
  • Objection: object to processing of your data for specific purposes, such as analytics.
  • Withdrawal of consent: where processing is based on your consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at velan@vkatos.com. We will respond within 5 business days.

8. DPDP compliance (India)

VKatOS is an Indian company and we comply with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act). Specifically:

  • We collect personal data only for defined, lawful purposes with your consent or another lawful basis.
  • We maintain a record of processing activities as required under the DPDP Act.
  • You have the right to nominate a representative to exercise data rights on your behalf.
  • Grievances may be raised with our Data Fiduciary representative by emailing velan@vkatos.com.

9. Cookies and analytics

Our website uses essential cookies required for the platform to function (authentication sessions, CSRF protection). We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

We may use privacy-preserving analytics to understand aggregate traffic patterns. No personally identifiable information is stored in analytics data. You may disable cookies in your browser settings; doing so may limit certain platform features.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will notify you by email (to the address on your account) and by posting a notice on the platform at least 14 days before the changes take effect. Your continued use of the platform after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

The version history and previous versions of this policy are available on request.
