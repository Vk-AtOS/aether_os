FAQ

Common Questions

Is there a contract?

No. We believe in value-based retention. You can cancel your subscription at any time.

How long does setup take?

Our standard deployment takes exactly 2 weeks from the date of setup fee payment to being live in production.

Does my data leave my network?

Never. VKatOS is designed to be deployed on your own hardware or VPC. We process data locally and only send encrypted health metrics to our monitoring center.

What if I need support?

We provide priority WhatsApp support from 9am to 9pm IST at +91 80562 38628. Enterprise clients have access to a 24/7 hotline.

Can I upgrade my plan?

Yes, at any time. If you upgrade mid-month, we adjust your billing pro-rata for the remaining days.

Is DPDP Act compliance included?

Yes. All VKatOS modules are built with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (2023) in mind, ensuring your enterprise stays compliant.
